A residual-driven digital twin for MALE UAV aero piston engines. A physics model runs in lockstep with the engine on the same inputs; the difference between them — the residual — is what every detector downstream reads. This document describes how the prototype is built, how each layer works, and what it deliberately refuses to claim.

01The Core Claim

Most engine-monitoring systems compare a sensor reading against a threshold. That works for failures and is nearly useless for degradation, because a cylinder can run thirty degrees hotter than it should and still sit well inside its redline — the reading is normal for the limit and abnormal for the conditions.

AEROTWIN is built on the second comparison. Every second:

The invariant

Nothing in the analytics stack reads a raw sensor value. The detector, the health index, the explanation and the advisory all consume residuals. That is the difference between a twin and a dashboard with a 3D model on it, and it is enforced structurally: the detector's feature vector is built from ChannelResidual objects, which do not expose an unnormalised reading.

Why per-cylinder asymmetry is the diagnostic basis

All four cylinders share an intake, an exhaust system, a cooling airflow and a crankshaft. Anything that changes the whole engine — a throttle movement, a climb, a hot day — moves all four together. A fault in one cylinder moves one of them relative to the other three. So the quantity that carries fault information is not CHT, and not even the CHT residual, but the dispersion of the four CHT residuals about their own median. This is why the system can localise to a cylinder at all, and why a corpus without per-cylinder instrumentation cannot support that claim (§09).

Two processes. The backend owns the twin and the truth; the frontend is a viewer with one exception — it can run a reduced model of its own when the backend is absent, and says so on every value.

The lockstep loop

The backend's clock runs at 5 Hz and advances time_scale ÷ 5 one-second samples per tick, so a 20× replay steps four samples per tick and a 200× demonstration steps forty. The loop is wrapped so a failure anywhere in the analytics stack degrades the diagnosis rather than stopping the stream — a ground station that goes silent is a worse failure than one reporting reduced capability. 

while running:
    if not paused and datalink:
        for _ in range(samples_this_tick):
            frame = source.read()          # 1 Hz sample, any source
            runtime.step(frame)            # physics -> residual -> estimator -> detector
    broadcast()                            # four topics, only what changed
    sleep(0.2 - elapsed)

03The Data Layer

The architectural centrepiece is sources/base.py. Every origin — a real corpus, the team corpus, the demonstration simulator, a live CAN bus — implements the same interface and emits the same frame shape. Everything downstream is written against that contract and never against a particular source.

Why this matters

Adapting AEROTWIN to a DRDO/VRDE test rig is one class. Not a rewrite, not a re-training, not a schema migration — one implementation of three methods. The claim is testable, and §03.2 is the test: a corpus with a different schema, different units and a different level of instrumentation was dropped in without touching anything downstream.

The demonstration simulator deserves a note. It is not a random number generator. It runs the same thermodynamic model the twin uses, but against a hidden truth carrying a unit-specific fingerprint (volumetric efficiency, cooling effectiveness and per-cylinder trim slightly off nominal — every real engine has one), a progressive degradation mechanism, thermal inertia, sensor noise and slow sensor bias. The twin is never told about any of it. The residual it computes is therefore a genuine emergent quantity rather than a scripted number.

03.2  The team corpus, and what it proved

The corpus from the team's data workstream (MONISHA-tech316/aerotwin-person1) is generated by a documented simulator — its own generator adds Gaussian noise to fixed baselines — so every frame is tagged SIMULATED and its state/fault columns are described as generator labels, never as maintenance findings.

A distinction the code enforces

A channel's registry provenance says whether it can be measured — that is a property of the contract. Whether it was depends on which source currently holds the live seat, and only the source descriptor knows that. Every REAL badge in the console passes through a single selector that consults the descriptor, so a badge can never end up on the output of the simulator. Two different questions, two different answers, one gate.

05The Physics Model

A lumped-parameter FGPiston-class model of a 180 hp, four-cylinder, horizontally opposed, air-cooled, fuel-injected engine (Lycoming IO-360 class). It consumes throttle, mixture, altitude, OAT and airspeed and produces an expectation for every modelled channel.

StageWhat it computes
AtmosphereDensity ratio from pressure altitude via the ISA relation (1 − 6.875e−6·h)4.2561
InductionManifold pressure from throttle position and ambient density; volumetric efficiency from RPM and MAP
CombustionIndicated power from air mass flow and mixture; brake power after friction; fuel flow from BSFC
ThermalHead heat rejection against cooling mass flow — a function of airspeed and density — giving CHT; exhaust enthalpy giving EGT
LubricationOil pressure from RPM; oil temperature from power and OAT
InertiaFirst-order lag applied identically to expectation and observation (τ ≈ 34 s CHT, 7 s EGT, 150 s oil)
Why the lag is applied to both sides

Cylinder heads and oil do not follow a power change instantly. If only the observation carried thermal inertia, every throttle transient would manufacture a residual that decayed over the next half-minute and the detector would fire on ordinary flying. Filtering both halves with the same time constants means a transient produces no residual at all — which is correct, because nothing is wrong.

Installing jsbsim switches the physics half from the in-process model to JSBSim itself, with no other code change. The physics backend reports which one is active on the Architecture screen.

06The Residual Engine

A raw difference is not comparable across channels: eight degrees of CHT and eight rpm are not the same size of surprise. Each channel therefore carries a scale — the standard deviation of its residual over observed healthy, steady-state operation — frozen once the baseline locks.

QuantitySymbolMeaning
Residualr = observed − expectedThe raw difference, in the channel's own unit
Normalisedz = r / scaleComparable across channels; the detector's input
DriftEWMA(r)Slow component — a developing fault, not a gust
PersistenceCUSUM(|z| − k)How long the deviation has held; separates a real drift from a spike
AsymmetryEWMA(r)ᵢ − medianPer-cylinder deviation from its three siblings — the localising quantity

The steady-state gate matters. Residuals are only admitted to the baseline and to the learned model while the operating point is steady, because a transient residual is a modelling artefact and training on it teaches the detector that artefacts are normal.

07State Estimation

Every engine leaves the factory slightly different, and those differences are not faults. The state estimator separates the two by fitting three slowly varying parameters that describe this engine's identity:

  • Volumetric efficiency — how well this engine breathes relative to nominal.
  • Cooling effectiveness — how well its cowling and baffles reject heat.
  • Per-cylinder trim — the fuel/air distribution across the four cylinders.

These are estimated from healthy operation and then held. A fault appears as a residual the estimator cannot absorb, which is exactly the definition wanted: a persistent, localised, unexplainable deviation. The estimator is gated — it reports LEARNING until it has enough steady healthy evidence and only then LOCKED, and while it is learning the detector's confidence is capped.

08Detection and Fusion

Two detectors, deliberately different in character, run on the same residual features.

08.1  The transparent baseline

A linear model over named residual features. It is not there because it is the most powerful thing available — it is there because a propulsion engineer can read it, argue with it, and change a coefficient. Every term has a physical meaning and a unit. When the system says a cylinder is deviating, this is the half that can be interrogated.

08.2  The learned model

AlgorithmIsolation Forest, 200 estimators (scikit-learn)
SupervisionUnsupervised — fitted on normality, not on labelled faults
Input9 residual features: CHT/EGT asymmetry, CHT drift, normalised z, persistence, trim divergence, EGT trim divergence, CHT/EGT agreement, CHT dispersion
Training set240 steady-state healthy residual vectors pooled across four cylinders, of this engine
Until thenReports UNTRAINED and contributes exactly zero to the fused score

Fitting on this engine's own healthy residuals — rather than on a fleet — means the model learns what normal looks like here, so a unit that has always run one cylinder slightly warm does not generate a permanent false positive.

08.3  Fusion

The physics baseline keeps the majority weight and an abstaining learned model changes nothing at all, so the fused score can never be worse-founded than the transparent half alone. A learned model that disagrees can raise or lower confidence; it cannot manufacture a detection on its own.

09Abstention and the Model Envelope

The system is designed to be able to say it does not know. There are two independent abstention paths.

09.1  Insufficient evidence

Where the residual is present but thin — not persistent enough, not seen across enough regimes, estimator not yet locked — the anomaly is returned with abstained: true and a stated reason, and the console renders MODEL ABSTENTION rather than a diagnosis. A mechanism is always named as LIKELY CONTRIBUTING, never as confirmed.

09.2  Outside the model envelope

This one was added because the team corpus exposed it. The expectation comes from a model calibrated for a 2,700 rpm, four-cylinder air-cooled aero engine. Hand it a corpus recorded from a different machine and every residual is enormous — not because anything is wrong with that engine, but because the model was not built for it. The health index collapsed to zero and the engine was reported CRITICAL.

The failure this prevents

That number would have been a lie. The residual was real, but it was a model mismatch residual, not evidence of a fault: nothing had been observed about that engine except that AEROTWIN's model was not built for it. Reporting a failing engine from that is precisely the failure mode this system exists to be immune to.

So the envelope is checked before anything is diagnosed:

VerdictConditionBehaviour
IN ENVELOPERPM within calibration, full channel coverageDiagnose normally
REDUCEDModel applies, instrumentation incompleteDiagnose what is covered; name what is not
OUT OF ENVELOPERPM beyond 1.15× rated, or coverage under 30%Abstain — no health verdict, no anomalies, no advisory

On abstention health_index is returned as null, not 0, and the gauge draws a dash rather than a zero. Absolute threshold alerts still fire: a redline is a redline whatever model is running.

10Explanation and Advisory

A score is not an answer. Every anomaly carries the evidence that produced it, so the operator sees the reasoning rather than a number:

FieldContent
Affected channel & cylinderWhich signal, on which cylinder
Actual / Expected / ResidualThe three numbers, in the channel's own unit
Trend & persistenceDrifting or stable; how many samples it has held
Regime coverageHow many distinct power/altitude regimes it has been seen in
Feature contributionsWhich residual features drove the score, and by how much
MechanismNamed as LIKELY CONTRIBUTING — e.g. early exhaust valve distress
ConfidenceCalibrated, or ABSTAINED with the reason

The advisory layer turns that into an action — an inspection recommendation scoped to a cylinder, with the serviceability outlook from the health trend. It is an advisory, not a maintenance authorisation, and the console says so.

11The Simulation Clock

Everything that moves because the simulation is moving derives from one object. Nothing else in the console owns a timer that advances simulation state. That single rule replaced an architecture in which the console had a second clock of its own — a 220 ms interval driving the in-browser model, which knew nothing about the paused flag and kept stepping through a pause.

StateMeaning
idleNothing loaded, nothing running
startingLoading data, initialising twin and detector — not yet advancing; each stage named on screen
runningThe clock is advancing
pausedHeld; index and time do not change
stopping / stoppedTorn down, restartable; computed state kept for inspection
completedThe source was exhausted; holds on the last real sample
errorStart-up or transport failed; the reason is on screen

11.1  Two transports, one state machine

LIVE commands the backend and follows the frames it sends. LOCAL drives the reduced in-browser model when no backend answers. The state machine, the controls and every panel behave identically; only the transport differs. This is what makes the full control surface work on a static deployment, where it previously did nothing at all.

11.2  What PAUSE holds

Pause is not a UI state. When it is pressed, the clock stops and with it: the dataset index, telemetry, the charts, the residual, the twin, anomaly processing, health, mission progress, the UAV's flight integration, the contrail, the piston and crankshaft animation, the cooling-airflow particles, and the narration timeline. Decorative hover motion continues; nothing simulation-driven does.

Three subtleties are handled explicitly.

  • A live frame already in flight when the command went out is dropped at the gate, so no number moves a beat after the press.
  • The console pauses optimistically — held on the press, not on the round trip — then settles onto the sample the backend actually stopped on. That figure is read after the tick in progress has finished, and off the same runtime object /telemetry/latest reports from, so the index the console settles onto and the frame it displays are the same sample by construction rather than by coincidence.
  • The status poll reconciles the console against the backend, and a status response already in flight when PAUSE was pressed comes back saying the server is running. Following it would silently undo the pause, so every local command stamps an epoch and a snapshot that predates one is ignored.

11.3  The control surface

The offered buttons are derived from the status rather than written out by hand, so PAUSE is never offered on an already-held simulation and STEP is never offered on a running one.

Authority

In LIVE mode the backend owns the clock, so it is the authority on whether anything is advancing. The status poll reconciles: a console showing a running simulation over a server paused from somewhere else is the same lie as a pause that does not pause, in the other direction.

The 3D stage

One never-unmounted canvas serves the corner card, the Command Center hero and the fullscreen mission view; it animates between those framings rather than remounting, so the aircraft is continuous across the transition. The engine assembly is a real four-cylinder layout — crankcase, four barrels with cooling fins, pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft, propeller flange — and its crank angle is integrated from RPM, so the pistons turn because the simulation is turning them. When the simulation holds, so do they.

If WebGL is unavailable the stage falls back to a 2D attitude view driven by the same flight model, so the same numbers are shown with a context every machine has. An error boundary guarantees a lost GL context degrades the visualisation rather than blanking the telemetry.

The narrated demonstration

RUN STORY plays the whole chain full screen over the aircraft in about ninety seconds — link, telemetry, twin sync, healthy engine, first deviation, residual growth, cylinder separation, estimator, detector, explanation, advisory. The narration is scripted; every number it shows is read live from the store at that moment, so it cannot state something the system is not actually reporting. It reads the sortie already in progress rather than rewinding it, and its pause control pauses the simulation it is narrating.

The demonstration path

  1. Open the console — the cinematic intro plays on first entry, skippable and replayable.
  2. Inspect the aircraft and the engine on /uav: physical, thermal, airflow, twin.
  3. Enter the Command Center. The sortie already in progress is adopted and shown.
  4. Press RUN STORY for the ninety-second narrated chain, or drive it manually.
  5. Watch the residual grow on /twin; watch cylinder 3 separate on /engine.
  6. Press PAUSE. Every number holds. Press STEP to walk it one second at a time.
  7. Open /anomalies for the evidence, /maintenance for the advisory.
  8. Open /dataset and press REPLAY on a team-corpus file to watch the twin abstain.

16Limitations

Stated here rather than left to be discovered.

AreaStateDetail
AirframeGAP The available real corpus is general-aviation (Cessna 172 class), not a MALE UAV. Same engine class — four-cylinder horizontally opposed, ~180 hp, air-cooled, fuel-injected — so the thermodynamics, failure modes and sensor channels transfer. Duty cycle and airframe do not.
VibrationSIMULATED Vibration and injection timing have no counterpart in any available corpus and are produced by the physics model. Tagged SIMULATED everywhere they appear.
Operational dataGAP No proprietary, operational or classified data is used or included. The repository contains none.
Physics modelSIMPLIFIED Lumped-parameter, not CFD or FEA. Calibrated for one engine class; outside that envelope the system abstains rather than extrapolating (§09.2).
Team corpusGENERATED Produced by a documented simulator, not measured. Bulk channels only — no per-cylinder instrumentation, so it cannot support cylinder-level localisation.
ValidationSYNTHETIC Metrics come from injected degradation, not labelled maintenance events on real hardware.
Live flightOUT OF SCOPE The SocketCAN deployment path is implemented as an interface; it is not exercised against a real engine bus in this build.
Standing commitment

Simulated data is never presented as measured, anywhere. Where evidence is thin the system reports abstention and the reason rather than guessing, and it names a likely contributing mechanism, never a confirmed failure. A single instance of a generated channel being mistaken for a measurement would discredit everything else in the project, which is why provenance is enforced in the type system and not by convention.
